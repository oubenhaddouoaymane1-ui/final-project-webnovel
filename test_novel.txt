Chapter 1: The Awakening

The morning sun cast long shadows across the ancient castle courtyard. Sir Aldric, a tall knight with brown hair and piercing blue eyes, stood at the gate, his silver armor gleaming in the light.

"Another peaceful day," he muttered, gripping his sword hilt.

From the towers above, Princess Elara watched him. Her golden hair cascaded down her shoulders as she leaned against the cold stone. She had known Aldric since childhood - he was her protector, her friend, perhaps more.

"Aldric!" she called down. "The king requests your presence."

He looked up, his expression softening when he saw her. "At once, Your Highness."

Chapter 2: The Council

The throne room was filled with nervous whispers. King Theron sat upon his throne, his aged face troubled. Beside him stood the court wizard, Meridian, his dark robes concealing ancient power.

"The dragon has been spotted near the eastern villages," Meridian announced, his voice carrying across the hall.

Murmurs of fear rippled through the crowd. Dragons had not been seen in these lands for a hundred years.

Aldric stepped forward. "Your Majesty, I volunteer to investigate and slay this beast."

The king's eyes showed concern. "It is a dangerous task, Sir Aldric."

"I am prepared, my king."

Elara rushed forward. "Father, let me go with him. I can use the ancient magic to help."

The king hesitated, but Meridian nodded. "The princess's abilities may be crucial in this quest."

And so the adventure began.
